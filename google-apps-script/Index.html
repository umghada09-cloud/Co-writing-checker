Smarter grading. Fairer marks.

A marking assistant for handwritten English writing. Photograph the pile, read the proposed marks, keep the final say.

  1. Import your gradebookThe Excel file you already keep. Classes and names come across in the order of your paper pile — no retyping.
  2. Photograph the papersTen at a time, in register order. Each one is read and given a mark against the Ministry rubric.
  3. Approve or change itEvery mark is yours. Eval learns from the ones you change, and writes them back into your own register.
